# P(max) as a Stage-1 term — RESULT

**Brief**: `docs/briefs/2026-09-30-pmax-stage1.md`, pre-registered at
`1a4cfaf`.
**Decision**: `F` adopted as v6.3.0 by user decision (see the end). Under
the pre-registered gates alone, `F` failed (targeted t +1.68 < 2).

## Change

`F` moves P(max) out of Stage 2 into the Stage-1 base margin as
`beta_max * P(max) * max(max_eligible_pct - kf_market_value, 0)`, with no
push. `N` drops the push and adds nothing. The switches are
`grabit_params["push"]` and `max_term_on`; `F` is the default since v6.3.0.

## Evidence

10 seeds, repeated grouped CV, all layers, one run on the Windows machine
(`python scripts/eval_pmax_stage1.py --seeds 10 --full`).

before `1a4cfaf` gives the same one-seed A1, so the gap is environmental
(ISSUES #62). All arms share folds, seeds and environment, so the paired
deltas below are valid. Absolute values are not comparable with v6.2.0.

## Decision

Under the pre-registered rule, `F` fails gate 1 and is rejected. Its A1 and
B1 gains (+0.008) are larger than the null expected in the brief, but they
do not reach t > 2 on the affected rows. They must not be adopted after the
fact. `F` against `N` was not a pre-registered comparison.

`N` against `R`: dSel +0.0042 >= 0 and B1 +0.0015 >= 0. The brief leaves
the deletion of the push to the user.

## User decision (2026-09-30)

The user accepted `F`. The change is a structural refactor: it removes the
Stage-2 push and moves P(max) into Stage 1. It is not a feature added for
performance, so the targeted gate does not apply. `F` passes the other gates.
Landed as v6.3.0; see `VERSION_HISTORY.md`.
