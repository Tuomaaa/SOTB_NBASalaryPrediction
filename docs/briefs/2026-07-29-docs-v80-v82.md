## Part 2 — METHODOLOGY

### The three-stage architecture

The model is now explicitly three stages, and CONTEXT.md needs the vocabulary
before METHODOLOGY can use it:

1. **Stage 1** — the player's value, unconstrained by any rule.
2. **Stage 2** — the CBA bounds that apply to the player himself: his max tier
   and the league minimum. Deterministic, needs no route. This is where the
   **push** lives, because it is the upward half of the same bound — the clip
   alone can only cap from above and cannot reach a max-worthy player the
   model prices below his ceiling.
3. **Stage 3** — the adjustment that only applies once the **signing route is
   known**: an extension is capped at its legal raise limit.

Composition lives in `src/model/stages.py` and all three consumers call it.

### The told-route convention, and the test that bounds it

Adopted 2026-07-26 and refined 2026-07-27. Told-route numbers go in the **same
column** as ex-ante numbers: the route is information the champion also had
and merely ignored. **v7.1x–v7.13x predate the convention** and every entry
from v8.0x must say so.

The refinement is the part that keeps it honest, and it is a per-route test:
*after being told the route, does the salary still require a non-trivial
computation?*

- **Extension — counts.** Told "he extended", you still compute 1.40 × prior
  pay (or the average-salary alternative) to land on Brunson's $34.94M.
- **Floor — does not count.** `floor_pct` sits within $0.13M of observed pay,
  so being told the route is being told the answer. The floor branch's told
  arm scored +0.046 and it measures recitation.

### The veteran-extension raise cap

120% under the 2017 CBA, 140% under the 2023 CBA, **or the same multiple of
the published Estimated Average Player Salary, whichever is greater**. Three
distinctions carry it and each was a way an earlier measurement failed: the
cap governs only the **first paying year**; **rookie-scale extensions are
capped by the tier instead**, classified by whether the previous season was a
rookie-scale season and never by a text keyword; and **Designated Veteran
extensions are exempt**. The multiplier keys on the **signing** season — seven
rows sit on exactly 1.200 and were all signed by 2022, six sit on exactly
1.400 and were all signed from 2023, with zero crossings.

### Missingness semantics

A player who did not play has an **undefined** usage rate, not a missing one.
46 full-table rows now carry `games = minutes = 0` and a `did_not_play`
status instead of a league-median fill. Availability uses the exact s/s−1/s−2
window and **skips unknown seasons with weight renormalisation** rather than
scoring them as zero; 2020-21 counts 72 games.
