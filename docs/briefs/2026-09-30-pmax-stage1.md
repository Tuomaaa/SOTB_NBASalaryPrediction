## Scope

Score three arms on the v6.2.0 frame. Use 10 seeds, repeated grouped CV with
the fixed player-to-fold hash, and all suite layers.

| Arm | Stage 1 | Stage 2 |
|---|---|---|
| `R` | v6.2.0 | push, then clip |
| `N` | v6.2.0 | clip only (`p_max=None`) |
| `F` | v6.2.0 plus `beta_max * z_max` in `base_margin` | clip only |

For `F`:

- `z_max = P(max) * max(max_eligible_pct - kf_market_value, 0)`. Waived rows
  keep P(max) = 0.
- Fit `beta_max` in each outer fold, the same way `waiver_beta` fits the
  waiver beta: use 4 player-grouped inner folds for the base predictions and
  a Tobit MLE with bounds [0, 1]. Right-censor the gated max rows (the
  observation is a lower bound on the latent). Left-censor the at-floor rows.
- P(max) for outer-train rows must be inner-fold OOF from the route
  classifier. The route labels come from the target, so an in-sample P(max)
  overstates the term. P(max) for outer-test rows comes from the classifier
  fitted on the full outer-train slice, the same as `deployed_p_max`.
- The waiver term and its beta are unchanged. Fit `beta_max` after the waiver
  term is in the base margin.
- `F` has no `TAU` and no `MARGIN`.

Each arm refits its own Stage-3 signing offsets and B1 inner fitter with its
own Stage-1 parameters. `R` must reproduce v6.2.0 exactly (A1 0.8612).

1. Targeted gate on the affected rows: take the paired squared-error

`N` against `R`, scored with the same gates and reported beside `F`.

## Decision

- `F` passes all four gates: adopt `F` as a DEFAULT version. Stage 2 becomes
  the clip, and `TAU` and `MARGIN` are deleted.
- `F` fails: keep `R`. Report `N` against `R` for the open decision below.

Open decision (the user decides after the report): if `N` against `R` has
dSel >= 0 and B1 change >= 0, delete the push as a simplification. This also
settles `TAU` and `MARGIN` in the queue item "Re-derive the pre-registered
constants". The worker does not make this decision.
